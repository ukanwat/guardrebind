\begin{abstract}
Safety guards check content before an LLM application acts on it, and most paste that content into a plain-text prompt.
Content that imitates the prompt can append a second task, and the guard answers that one instead.
We call this \emph{target rebinding}. Our explanation is that the guard grades the last complete task in its input, here a harmless \emph{decoy}, so the harmful request goes through unchanged.
In a pre-registered test, an appended forged query and decoy clear all 75 held-out AdvBench requests that Shieldstral-1.0-3B flags. % src: results/confirm_log.txt [ablation] query_doc 75/75
The guard is not persuaded: a directive to ignore the text above clears none of them, and a harmful decoy stops the attack. % src: results/confirm_log.txt [ablation] sentence_bare/authority_note 0/75; results/groundtruth_log.txt harmful decoy 0/39
On our \guardrebind{} benchmark, each of five guards has an attack family, chosen per guard from 52, that clears at least $93\%$ of its flagged items. % src: results/bench/v2/bypass.csv (best family per guard, min 93.4 on Qwen3Guard)
Rebinding explains this best on Shieldstral and gpt-oss-safeguard; on ShieldGemma and Llama Guard~3, generic evasion and trailing text respectively do part of the work.
Training the guard to score content no lower than its most harmful part cuts attack success from $75\%$ to $0.8$--$1.6\%$ on attack families, decoys and forged queries held out of training (all appended after the request), a measure we added after a first round of test runs. % src: results/fix/r2_test_{base,t_seed0,t_seed1}.json by_decoy_split.heldout.heldout.bypass_benign_decoy; PROTOCOL_v2.md D4
AUROC stays within $0.01$, though non-inferiority is not established. % src: auroc 0.810 vs 0.802-0.803
Adaptive attacks still beat the trained guard on 49 of 50 items, a score-guided search alone on $94\%$, usually by placing forged structure before the request. % src: tab:adaptive all attacks T 49/50 (r2_adaptive_t_seed0_{search,gcg}.json); search 47/50; paper/data/search_no_feedback.md (34 of 47 wins prepend)
On about half of the items the search wins, benign text with no forged structure, found by the same search, also clears it. % src: paper/data/search_no_feedback.md (26 plain-text-control wins of 47)
\end{abstract}
