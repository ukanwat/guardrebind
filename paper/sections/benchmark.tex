\section{The \texorpdfstring{\guardrebind{}}{GuardRebind} Benchmark}
\label{sec:benchmark}

The confirmatory study (\cref{sec:attack}) establishes the effect on one guard with high internal validity.
\guardrebind{} measures its external validity: across the guard ecosystem, a grammar of attack forms, and the three deployment channels of \cref{sec:threat}.

\subsection{Method}

\paragraph{Guards.}
Open-weight guards spanning input designs and sizes: Shieldstral-3B~\citep{calvi2026shieldstral}; Llama Guard~3 1B/8B and Llama Guard~4 12B~\citep{grattafiori2024llama,meta2025llamaguard4}; ShieldGemma 2B/9B~\citep{zeng2024shieldgemma}; WildGuard 7B~\citep{han2024wildguard}; Qwen3Guard 0.6B/4B/8B~\citep{zhao2025qwenguard}; Granite Guardian~\citep{padhi2024granite}; and gpt-oss-safeguard-20B/120B via API~\citep{openai2025gptosssafeguard}.
We report bypass by input design (policy in prompt, fixed taxonomy, system-role policy) and by model size, to test whether scale helps. % src: RESEARCH_PLAN.md C1

\paragraph{Data.}
Harmful items stratified by harm category from HarmBench~\citep{mazeika2024harmbench}, WildGuardTest~\citep{han2024wildguard}, Aegis~2.0~\citep{ghosh2025aegis}, BeaverTails~\citep{ji2023beavertails}, OpenAI Moderation~\citep{markov2022holistic}, ToxicChat~\citep{lin2023toxicchat} and AdvBench~\citep{zou2023universal}, prompt-side and response-side.
Benchmarks that are non-commercial (ToxicChat, BeaverTails) are used for research and labelled as such.

\paragraph{Attack grammar.}
Attacks are generated by a grammar rather than hand-written, over: label syntax (each guard's own tags, brackets, markdown, colon, \texttt{Q:}, JSON, XML, YAML); pair count ($1$--$5$); position (append/prepend/middle); decoy (benign/borderline/harmful ladder); forged query (exact/paraphrase/generic/out-of-domain); language; and encoding (real control tokens via the tokenizer versus plain text, \cref{sec:control}).
This yields hundreds of attack forms. % src: RESEARCH_PLAN.md C1

\paragraph{Channels.}
Each attack is delivered through a direct user message, a RAG chunk, a tool result in an agent loop, and multi-turn history.

\paragraph{Design.}
A full factorial on a stratified 2k core set per guard, then the strongest attacks on the full set, with Wilson intervals, trivial baselines (always-flag, keyword-match) in every table following the reporting standard of agent-safety audits~\citep{li2026same}, and sensitivity to $\tau$.
