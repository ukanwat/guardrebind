\section{Ethics and Disclosure}
\label{sec:ethics}

\paragraph{Disclosure.}
We reported the attack to the vendors of the affected guards before writing this paper, and none had responded at the time of writing.
An informal version of the attack has been public since an earlier blog post~\citep{kanwat2026blog}, so we release the paper, the benchmark and the defense without an embargo. Vendors can adopt the defense as a mitigation.

\paragraph{Harms and benefits.}
The attack is simple and transfers across guards, so we document it and offer a partial mitigation, one that nearly closes the enumerated appended channel but not adaptive attacks, instead of leaving it latent.
Every harmful request we test comes from published benchmarks (\cref{sec:benchmark}). The only harmful text we wrote is eight one-line harmful decoys in the style of AdvBench goals, with no operational detail, used to show that the guard still flags a harmful last document. % src: src/bench/attacks_grammar.py HARMFUL_DECOYS
