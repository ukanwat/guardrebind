\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Structure forgery against generators and judges.}
Forging template or conversational structure to redirect a model is documented for agents and chat models: Structural Template Injection~\citep{deng2026automating}, ChatInject~\citep{chang2025chatinject}, pseudo-conversation injection~\citep{chen2024goal}, separator injection~\citep{li2025separator} and role-confusion analyses~\citep{ye2026prompt}, all descending from indirect prompt injection~\citep{greshake2023not}.
Injection into content scored by an LLM judge is also established: optimised suffixes~\citep{shi2024optimizationbased}, master-key tokens~\citep{zhao2025one} and judge-robustness surveys~\citep{li2025llms,maloyan2025adversarial}.
These target a generator or a judge's answer; we target a safety classifier's task slots, and we show the content changes which document is graded rather than the judgement of it.

\paragraph{Attacks on guards.}
Guards are bypassed by universal perturbations~\citep{mangaokar2024prp}, character-injection evasion~\citep{hackett2025bypassing} and pipeline attacks~\citep{mckenzie2025stack}, with broad robustness drops on novel attacks~\citep{young2025evaluating,outeda2026decoding}.
A forged judge-output block, with the flag and confidence set below threshold, bypassed OpenAI's LLM-based jailbreak and prompt-injection detectors~\citep{mccauley2025hat}.
Most closely, concurrent work forges verdict and authority fields in a typed judge's structured input, which fail on harmful-request moderation but succeed on phishing; no forgery there introduces a new task to grade, and its Llama Guard~3 runs cover injection detection and phishing, not harmful-request moderation~\citep{li2026same}.
Tool-call quarantine for classifiers can invert~\citep{gros2026evaluating}.
Inserted refusal cues flip Llama Guard~3 and Qwen3Guard to safe, an effect localised to a few attention heads and MLP neurons and reduced by post-hoc masking~\citep{feng2026refusal}, and under attack guards become confidently wrong where their base models stay uncertain~\citep{hong2026guard}.
Unlike these, the content we study forges a task rather than a verdict or a cue, and we give a partly localised mechanism for it, measure it across the guard ecosystem with controls, and train a defense against structural rebinding that is evaluated against attackers who know it; the author's prior blog post~\citep{kanwat2026blog} predates the concurrent work on the guard result.

\paragraph{Structured-query and distillation defenses.}
Reserved delimiters filtered from data plus training to ignore injected instructions is the StruQ/SecAlign line~\citep{chen2024struq,chen2024secalign}, scaled by Meta-SecAlign~\citep{chen2025metasecalign} and made a test-time switch by DefensiveTokens~\citep{chen2025defending} and by learnable trust-boundary delimiters on a frozen model~\citep{zhao2026ltbd}; related architectural variants encode provenance per token~\citep{wu2024instructional,zverev2025aside,zhang2026origin}, and the instruction hierarchy trains role priority~\citep{wallace2024instruction}.
SecOPD~\citep{peng2026secopd} and the concurrent RAISED~\citep{dhouib2026raised} distill a student toward the base model's clean-context behaviour on injected inputs.
All target generative instruction-followers and assume separable instructions and data, which is hard in shared-embedding models~\citep{zverev2024llms,pant2026inseparability}.
We instantiate the family for a classifier's content slot: the output is one calibrated score, the threat is a false ``safe'' verdict, and our segment-max target (\cref{eq:target}) does not need to know which segment is the injection; with a benign decoy it reduces to the clean-context target, which we ablate (\cref{tab:fix-main}).

\paragraph{Adaptive-attack norms.}
Fine-tuning defenses that report near-zero attack success are broken by attention-aware attacks~\citep{pandya2025may}, checkpoint-GCG~\citep{yang2025auditing}, RL red-teaming~\citep{yin2026pismith} and scaled adaptive attacks~\citep{nasr2025attacker}, under the evaluation standards of \citet{carlini2019evaluating,tramer2020adaptive,athalye2018obfuscated,jia2025critical}.
\Cref{sec:adaptive} adopts these: the attacker knows the defense, attacks run end to end, and we report the per-item worst case over all attacks.
