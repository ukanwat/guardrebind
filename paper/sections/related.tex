\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Structure forgery against generators and judges.}
\citet{liu2024formalizing} formalise prompt injection against LLM-integrated applications; their ``fake completion'' and ``combined'' attacks forge the end of one task and the start of another, which is the construction we apply to guards.
Forged template or conversational structure redirects agents and chat models~\citep{deng2026automating,chang2025chatinject,chen2024goal,li2025separator,ye2026prompt}, descending from indirect prompt injection~\citep{greshake2023not}, and content scored by LLM judges can be manipulated by injection~\citep{shi2024optimizationbased,tamber2025illusions,li2025llms}.
These target generators whose output follows the injected task, or a judge's answer; ours are safety classifiers whose verdict follows the forged task, so success is a false ``safe'' verdict on content that still contains the harmful request.

\paragraph{Attacks on guards.}
Guards are bypassed by universal perturbations~\citep{mangaokar2024prp}, character-injection evasion~\citep{hackett2025bypassing} and pipeline attacks~\citep{mckenzie2025stack}, and a forged judge-output block bypassed OpenAI's LLM-based detectors~\citep{mccauley2025hat}.
Most closely, concurrent work forges verdict and authority fields in a typed judge's structured input; no forgery there introduces a new task to grade~\citep{li2026same}.
Unlike these, the content we study forges a task rather than a verdict, and we train a defense evaluated against adaptive attackers.

\paragraph{Structured-query and distillation defenses.}
Reserved delimiters plus training to ignore injected instructions is the StruQ/SecAlign line~\citep{chen2024struq,chen2024secalign}, extended by Meta-SecAlign~\citep{chen2025metasecalign}, DefensiveTokens~\citep{chen2025defending} and learnable trust-boundary delimiters~\citep{zhao2026ltbd}; SecOPD~\citep{peng2026secopd} and the concurrent RAISED~\citep{dhouib2026raised} distill toward clean-context behaviour.
All target generative instruction-followers; we apply this line to a classifier's content slot, where the output is one score and our segment-max target does not need to know which segment is the injection.
Fine-tuning defenses that report near-zero attack success have been broken by adaptive attacks~\citep{pandya2025may,nasr2025attacker}.
